About Us

Form Matters

An independent editorial publication dedicated to the thoughtful discussion of interior design, architecture, and the built environment. We believe good design is not about luxury—it's about clarity, function, and the quiet poetry of well-considered space.

Our Mission

Form Matters exists to bridge the gap between professional design discourse and the everyday reader. We publish long-form articles, practical guides, and critical perspectives that help people make better decisions about their own homes—and understand the forces shaping our cities.

In a media landscape saturated with fleeting trend reports and sponsored content, we remain committed to independent, researched, and beautifully written journalism. Every article is crafted with care, grounded in real expertise, and designed to be useful for years—not just news cycles.

We cover interior design, residential architecture, material innovation, lighting, space planning, and the intersection of design with sustainability and culture. Our writers are architects, designers, historians, and journalists who bring both practical knowledge and critical perspective to every piece.

What We Value

◈

Independence

We are reader-supported and editorially independent. We do not accept sponsored posts, and our recommendations are never for sale.

◈

Depth over Speed

We take time to research, interview, and fact-check. Our articles are built to last—not to chase algorithms.

◈

Practical Beauty

We celebrate design that works: spaces that function, materials that age gracefully, and ideas that improve daily life.

◈

Accessibility

Good design writing should be clear, jargon-free, and welcoming—whether you're a professional or a curious homeowner.

Editorial Team

Form Matters is produced by a small, dedicated team of writers and editors based across Europe and North America.

EM

Elena Marchetti

Editor-in-Chief

Elena is an architect and writer based in Milan. She has spent fifteen years documenting residential design across Europe and Japan, with a particular focus on material culture and spatial calm.

JR

James Reyes

Senior Editor, Architecture

James is a trained architect and former studio critic. He writes about adaptive reuse, sustainable building envelopes, and the evolving relationship between cities and their inhabitants.

SC

Sofia Chen

Materials & Interiors Editor

Sofia is a designer and material researcher. She explores the stories behind the surfaces we live with—from lime plaster to reclaimed timber—and champions craft-based approaches.

MK

Mira Kapoor

Contributing Writer, Lighting

Mira is a lighting designer and educator. Her work focuses on daylighting, human-centric lighting, and the role of light in shaping how we feel in interior spaces.

Editorial Standards

We hold ourselves to rigorous standards of accuracy, transparency, and integrity. Our editorial process includes:

  • ✓ Independent research and expert review for every technical article.
  • ✓ Clear separation between editorial content and any commercial partnerships.
  • ✓ Full disclosure of sources, affiliations, and potential conflicts of interest.
  • ✓ A commitment to correcting errors promptly and transparently.
  • ✓ Respect for intellectual property and fair attribution of images and ideas.

Contact & Submissions
